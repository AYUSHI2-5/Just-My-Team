What is Just My Team?

Just My Team is a dedicated platform designed for real estate agents and administrators. It brings core daily workflows together in an intuitive interface: manage your network, list properties, communicate in direct or group chats, and maintain personal notes.

Platform Roles

  • Agent Role: Manage network, view and list properties, chat with other agents, and organize personal notes.
  • Admin Role: Manage platform-wide settings and membership plans.
