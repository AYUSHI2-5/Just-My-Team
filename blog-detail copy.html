Getting Started with the Agent Dashboard & Network

By Just My Team Support • Platform Overview
TABLE OF CONTENTS
01 Dashboard Overview 02 Managing Your Network 03 Properties & Notes

1. Dashboard Overview

The Agent Dashboard is your central hub on Just My Team. Once you sign in, you can see an overview of your activity, monitor network status, and quickly access core features including properties, chat, and personal notes.

2. Managing Your Network

From the profile and network section, agents can view connected agents, inspect detailed profiles, and explore the hierarchical Agent Tree. You can also invite new agents using email validation.

3. Properties & Notes

Easily create, view, edit, and list properties associated with your account with dynamic state and city selection. Keep your personal notes organized with date filtering and export options.
