FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick and clear answers about platform features, user roles, authentication, and communication.

To register, visit the Register page, enter your referral code, fill in your personal details including state and city, and create a password.
Referral validation verifies the referral information provided during registration before an agent can complete the account creation process.
Use the Forgot Password option and complete the Email OTP verification process to recover access to your account.
The Agent Network allows agents to view their connected agents and access their detailed profiles.
The Agent Tree provides a hierarchical visualization of an agent's referral network.
Agents can invite new agents to join their network through email validation.
Agents can create, view, edit, and manage properties associated with their account.
Agents can communicate one-to-one with other agents through direct messaging.
Agents can create and manage group conversations, including the option to enable owner-only messaging.
Agents can create, read, update, and delete personal notes. Notes can also be filtered by date and exported.
Both Agents and Admins can view available membership plans, while only Admins can create, edit, and delete membership plans.
Users can view unread notifications, check unread counts, and mark notifications as read.
The platform uses dynamic state and city data for properties, profiles, and registration.
